CEO Story

Susanto Liman, the visionary behind MR.COFFEE, believes coffee is more than a beverage—it’s an experience, a connection, and an art. Inspired by his family’s kitchen, where conversations flourished over coffee, he set out to create a space where people gather and enjoy artisanal brews.


His journey took him across renowned coffee regions, learning from expert roasters and farmers. With years of dedication, he founded MR.COFFEE—a brand rooted in authenticity, community, and sustainability. Today, MR.COFFEE is a beloved café, embodying warmth, creativity, and a passion for excellence.

— Susanto Liman

Our Vision

Our vision is to be more than just a coffee shop—we aim to be a global community, where coffee becomes the medium for inspiration, creativity, and togetherness.

We envision MR.COFFEE expanding its reach while staying true to its core values: quality, authenticity, and sustainability.

Through innovative approaches, meaningful collaborations, and a deep-rooted passion for coffee, we strive to make MR.COFFEE a place where every cup serves as an invitation—to slow down, appreciate the moment, and connect with those around us.

Our Mission

At MR.COFFEE, our mission is simple yet profound: to create a space where coffee lovers can experience exceptional brews, meaningful conversations, and a genuine sense of belonging.

We are committed to sourcing the finest beans, crafting the perfect cup, and fostering a welcoming atmosphere that turns strangers into friends.

Our goal is to celebrate coffee as a bridge—connecting people, cultures, and stories—while maintaining ethical and sustainable practices in every aspect of our business.

The Story of MR.COFFEE

Born from a deep love for coffee and the connections it inspires, MR.COFFEE was more than just an idea—it was a heartfelt journey. Our founder, Susanto Liman, grew up surrounded by the comforting aroma of freshly brewed coffee, where every sip told a story and every cup sparked meaningful conversations.

With passion in his heart, he began an incredible journey across the globe—exploring renowned coffee regions, learning from master roasters, and embracing the artistry behind every bean.

His dream wasn’t just to build a coffee shop—it was to craft a community space. A place where ideas bloom, friendships thrive, and every detail—from ethically sourced beans to precise brewing—speaks of quality, sustainability, and love for the craft.

Today, MR.COFFEE stands as a warm haven for coffee lovers. It's not just about caffeine; it’s about creating moments, inspiring creativity, and bringing people together—one cup at a time.
